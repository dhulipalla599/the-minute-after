## Description (paste into the KDP description field)

We all have those moments. You know the right thing, and you feel you can
do nothing about it. Someone is pressured in a meeting. Someone helpless is
treated badly. The room goes quiet, and you go quiet with it.

The Minute After is about the minute after that moment.

Ten everyday stories: a pressured coworker, a guard shouted at in a lobby,
a meeting where nobody speaks, a new joiner nobody helps, an angry
customer, blame landing on the wrong person, a sharp reply at home, a
stranger in the queue, a burnt-out friend. Each one shows how one minute of
thought and one small decent act can make the day better for everyone in
the room.

Not heroes. Ordinary people, making one slightly better choice in difficult
moments. Including, in the last chapter, the day the difficult one was you.

One minute of thought, one small decent act, and the day gets better for
everyone.
