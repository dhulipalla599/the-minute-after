I Know, But I Can Do Nothing

But you can make it better.

by Venkata Sandeep Dhullipalla

We all have those moments. You know the right thing, and you feel you can do nothing about it. Someone is pressured in a meeting. Someone helpless is treated badly. The room goes quiet, and you go quiet with it.

This book is about the minute after that moment. One minute of thought, one small decent move, and the day gets better, not just for one person, but for everyone in the room.

Each chapter is one everyday situation, told as a short story: the moment, the one-minute pause, the small better move, and why it worked.
